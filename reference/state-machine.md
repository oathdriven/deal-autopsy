# The state machine: 7 stages, 7 gates

The diagnostic ladder. Source framework: CommitToClose (https://committoclose.com),
"sales is a state machine, not a script." Seven states, indexed from 0. Every stage is
insurance for a later stage: skip one and you pay for every objection at full price,
later, when the truth is expensive.

For each stage below: what happens there, and the GATE, the observable condition in a
transcript that marks it PASSED. The gate is what the coroner checks. Not whether the
stage was attempted; whether the gate cleared.

---

## stage-0 · lurk (recon before contact)

15 minutes of public research before the call, woven in naturally during it. Weave,
never recite: nobody trusts a stranger who reads their file out loud, but everybody
remembers the one caller who did their homework.

**GATE:** at least one researched, prospect-specific fact surfaces naturally in the
call and the prospect engages with it. Evidence reads like personalization, not a
recitation.

## stage-1 · rapport (find common ground, then land the frame)

1 to 2 minutes of small talk to find real overlap (never weather). Then the doctor
frame: "Think of me as your business doctor. I am going to ask you some questions so I
can diagnose what is going on and prescribe what you need. If I cannot help you, I will
refer you to a specialist. Does that make sense?"

**GATE:** the frame is stated AND the prospect agrees to it out loud. The yes is the
license for every question in stage-2. Questions asked without this license read as
interrogation.

## stage-2 · diagnosis (the frame licensed these questions, use them)

A doctor does not prescribe before examining. What brought you in. Are you happy with
your results. How long has this been a problem. What have you tried. Is there a partner
or spouse who should be on this call. Then the magic wand: "It is now 1 year from today.
Where is your business, that would make you happy?"

**GATE:** 3 conditions, all visible in the transcript: (a) the prospect names a pain in
their own words, (b) the prospect describes the future state they want, (c) every
decision-maker is confirmed present or accounted for.

## stage-3 · COMMITMENT (the stage everyone skips; the brand)

Before anything is presented: "Real quick, before I present my solution... if
everything makes sense on the call today, would you be opposed to moving forward at the
end of this call?" The only time no means yes. A no is safe to say, so they say it, and
now there is a spade in the seller's pocket for every objection that comes later.

**GATE:** the would-you-be-opposed question is asked BEFORE any presenting, and the
prospect gives a clear no (meaning: not opposed). A mumble, an "ummm sure", or a subject
change is FAKED, not passed. Presenting anything before this gate is the most common
death in the taxonomy.

## stage-4 · present (never demo the tech)

A roadmap in steps or phases, never a feature tour. "Where do you already fall?" They
answer. The steps they have not reached ARE the offer. Nobody argues with their own pin.

**GATE:** the prospect places THEMSELVES on the roadmap. Their pin, their words. A
monologue about features, however well received, does not clear this gate.

## stage-5 · test-close (surface the truth before the price)

"How does everything look?" "Do you see this NOT working for your business?" Real
objections show up here, where they cost nothing; the lies start after the price. If
they jump to "what is the price", isolate: "Other than price, are there any other
questions or concerns, or is price the only thing you need to know?" Flush the whole
stack first.

**GATE:** the objection stack is explicitly flushed (an "any other questions or
concerns" style sweep gets a clear answer) BEFORE any number is said.

## stage-6 · close (then shut up)

"It costs $X,XXX to get started. What do you want to do next?" Then silence. Not
another word. Done right, the prospect closes themselves.

**GATE:** the price is stated, the closing question is asked, and the seller stays
silent until the prospect responds.

## The objections branch (hotfix, only if needed)

Objections are not a stage; they are a side branch that a clean run never visits. Three
handlers cover everything: the spade (call out the contradiction plainly, leading with
"I'm confused"), isolation (grab the first objection, flush the rest of the stack before
answering), and the 1-to-10 scale (they dictate their own closing conditions). For the
coroner, the branch matters diagnostically: an objection handled WITHOUT a stage-3
commitment in the bank is being handled without a spade in the pocket, which is why
skipping stage-3 shows up later as unhandleable objections. The objection is the
symptom. The empty pocket is the cause.

## Why order usually matters, and when it does not

Each gate is insurance consumed by a later stage: the frame (1) licenses the exam (2),
the exam (2) makes the commitment ask (3) reasonable, the commitment (3) is the spade
for objections after the presentation (4), the flush (5) makes the close (6) safe. A
seller can clear gates out of the usual order and live, IF each gate still clears before
the stage that depends on it runs. The kill condition is a dependent stage running
uninsured. That is what the coroner looks for.
