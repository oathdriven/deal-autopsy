# Failure modes: the named deaths

The taxonomy the coroner rules from. Each entry: the name, the stage it kills at, what
it looks like in a transcript, and the symptoms it throws downstream. Rule the death by
its name when it matches; say so plainly when it matches none.

---

## stage-0 deaths

**The blank file.** No recon was done. The call opens generic and stays generic; the
seller asks questions the website answers. Downstream: prospect treats the seller as a
number because the seller treated them as one. Rarely fatal alone; usually co-occurs
with a later skip. Only rule it the cause when the prospect visibly disengages early
over being commoditized.

**The cold read.** Research was done and then recited like a file. "I saw on your
LinkedIn that..." 3 times in 2 minutes. Downstream: creepy replaces prepared; rapport
never forms.

## stage-1 deaths

**The weather report.** Small talk happens, common ground never does, and the frame is
never landed. The call has no doctor. Downstream: every diagnostic question lands as
nosy, answers stay shallow, no pain surfaces.

**The unlicensed exam.** Questions get asked without the frame's yes. Reads as
interrogation. Downstream: guarded one-line answers, the prospect takes conversational
control and starts interviewing the seller.

## stage-2 deaths

**The premature pitch.** The seller starts presenting before diagnosing anything. The
most violent death in the taxonomy: with a sharp prospect it ends in a hang-up inside
minutes; with a polite one it ends in "send me some info." Downstream: everything.
There is no pain on the table, so the pitch has nothing to attach to and price has
nothing to be weighed against.

**The missing decision-maker.** Nobody confirmed who decides. Downstream: the call runs
clean and dies at the close as "I need to talk to my wife / partner", which is then
unspadeable because it might even be true.

**The skipped wand.** Pain gets named but the future state never does. Downstream: the
roadmap in stage-4 has no destination to point at, so the presentation is a tour, not a
path.

## stage-3 deaths

**The unearned demo.** THE signature death, the stage everyone skips. The seller
presents to a prospect who never answered the would-you-be-opposed question. Downstream:
every objection after the presentation is met without a spade in the pocket. The
prospect stalls, ghosts, or "thinks about it", and the seller has nothing to call back
to. If the transcript shows presenting with no commitment ask before it, look hard here
before anywhere else.

**The yes-ask.** The seller asks for a yes ("can I get your commitment that...") instead
of offering the safe no. Survival instinct spikes; the prospect hedges ("ummm, sure?").
The gate reads attempted but FAKED: a hedge is not a commitment, and the spade it leaves
in the pocket is made of paper.

## stage-4 deaths

**The feature tour.** Tech gets demoed, features get listed, the roadmap never appears.
Downstream: the prospect evaluates the product instead of their own path, which invites
comparison shopping and "we already have something like this."

**The unpinned prospect.** A roadmap is shown but the prospect is never asked where they
fall on it. Downstream: it stays the seller's map, not theirs; nothing binds them to the
gap the offer fills.

## stage-5 deaths

**The unflushed stack.** The seller goes to price with objections still hidden.
Downstream: the objections surface AFTER the number, where every one of them is
negotiating leverage and half of them are lies.

**The naked price.** The prospect asks "so what does it cost?" and the seller just
answers it, no isolation. Downstream: same as the unflushed stack, plus conversational
control transfers to the prospect for the rest of the call.

## stage-6 deaths

**The nervous mouth.** Price is stated and the seller keeps talking: justifying,
discounting, adding bonuses. The silence that closes deals never happens. Downstream:
the prospect learns the price is soft and stalls to test it.

**The unasked close.** The call simply ends. Recap, thanks, "I'll send this over."
Nobody asked anybody to do anything. Downstream: a warm ghost. Often the visible tail of
an earlier skip; check stages 3 and 5 before ruling it the cause.

## Cross-cutting reads (symptoms that point backward)

These are the things sellers bring in as "the reason it died." They are almost never the
cause. Where to look instead:

| The seller says it died as...            | Look first at                              |
|------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| "Let me think about it"                  | stage-5 (unflushed stack), then stage-3    |
| "I need to talk to my wife / partner"    | stage-2 (missing decision-maker)           |
| A price objection                        | stage-2 (no pain = no value scale), then 5 |
| Ghosted after the demo                   | stage-3 (the unearned demo)                |
| "Follow up in 2 weeks"                   | stage-3 (no spade to call the stall out)   |
| "Send me some info"                      | stage-2 (nothing diagnosed to want)        |
| Objection loop, call ran long and died   | stage-5 (no isolation, stack never flushed)|
| Prospect took over with questions        | stage-1 (frame never landed)               |

The pattern under the whole table: the announced cause of death is the last symptom,
and the real death is upstream at the first gate that never cleared.
