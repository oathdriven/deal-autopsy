## Why these examples are trustworthy

The seller on these tapes closes for a living and lost these calls ON PURPOSE, one
deliberate mistake per call, selling normally everywhere else. That design matters for
reading the autopsies: each transcript contains exactly one planted cause plus the
natural symptom cascade it produced, so you can check the diagnostician's ruling
against a known ground truth. The same seller has also beaten the same hardest persona
straight up (that winning tape is the control group in reference/benchmarks.md), so
the losses are not skill noise. They are controlled failures.
